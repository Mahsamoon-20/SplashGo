Got Questions?
Frequently Asked
Do you bring your own water and power? ▾

Yes! We bring everything — our own water supply, power generator, and all cleaning products. You don't need to provide anything.

How long does each service take? ▾

Basic Wash takes about 45–60 minutes. Signature Wash is 1.5–2 hours. Full Detail can take 3–5 hours depending on the vehicle's condition.

Is the $20 deposit refundable? ▾

Yes! If you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment, your deposit is fully refunded. No questions asked.

What if it rains on my booking day? ▾

We'll reach out to reschedule if the weather is bad. Your deposit automatically moves to the new date.

Do you service all vehicle types? ▾

We handle sedans, coupes, SUVs, trucks, vans, and minivans. Just add the SUV/Truck add-on when booking a larger vehicle.

How do I pay the remaining balance? ▾

After the job is done, you can pay the remaining balance by e-Transfer, credit card, or cash — whatever works best for you.
